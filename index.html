Who ATIK is

A central, inclusive professional forum for a developing insolvency ecosystem.

ATIK was formed in response to the need for a professional, centralised and inclusive association for Kenya, particularly as the country's insolvency regime remains relatively new and jurisprudence continues to develop.

The Association provides a forum for practitioners to network, share know-how, engage the Office of the Official Receiver and the Judiciary, develop younger professionals and represent members' views on legislative change.

Built by the profession

A forum for practitioners, the Official Receiver, the Judiciary and other stakeholders.

Continental context

ATIK joins a small group of dedicated African associations advancing turnaround and insolvency practice.

The leadership imperative

Business distress is not merely a legal event.

It is a test of governance, capital and confidence.

How distress is managed determines whether value is preserved, jobs are protected and confidence in the market is strengthened, or lost.

01. Enterprise

Preserve viable businesses and productive assets.

02. Capital

Improve recovery, restructuring and allocation decisions.

The turnaround and insolvency profession is part of Kenya's economic infrastructure.

03. People

Protect jobs, livelihoods and institutional capability.

04. Confidence

Support trust in lending, investment and the rule of law.
